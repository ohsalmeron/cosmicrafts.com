# Cosmicrafts Overview

## Technical Foundation

Our platform is built on a high-performance, cost-effective cloud-native architecture:

- **Performance Core**: Matchmaking, lobbies, player progression, and in-game ledgers are handled by a high-efficiency Rust backend running in a secure FreeBSD jail. Valkey powers our memory storage, guaranteeing sub-millisecond database updates and zero per-transaction costs.
- **Unified Accounts**: One World of Unreal Identity (WOU-ID) account — email or social login — carries your profile, progress, and inventory across every Cosmicrafts title.
- **Lower Costs**: This setup costs a fraction of what we were burning before. That means we can keep delivering instead of watching overhead eat the runway.
